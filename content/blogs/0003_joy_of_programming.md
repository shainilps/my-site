I mean people have forgotten the joy of programming. technically this is not applicable to everyone
because there are two types of programmers. one who treat it as craftmanship, another who dont. well there
nothing bad with not really caring about craftmanship to be honest. recently I was also burned out
by the programming and I was just finishing job and played some games on freetime.
though now it's quite different I got my interest back, like I was in the beginning. learning new stuff
always excited me. I think its cool to know about many things. even if its useless. Like I have emacs in
my machine just becuase I wanted to write lisp, and I know some Haskell too (a very good language to express problems).
I know some rust too, go is my #1 language though because I use it daily for work.

When I'm writing this blog, I was learning about linux namespace and then when lenaring network namespace I got sidetracked by broadcast
and mutlicast working and doing some experiments with it using udp. That's just how I am.

most of people nowadays use it. and neovim have become quite the editor. At the time of writing this I'm using
neovim 13 pre release version(latest commit). Why you may ask? using neovim itself have some disadvantages that
it breaks config and blah blah. during my neovim usage of 1 year there are only two times that a config broke or you could
say plugin. one was neovim treesitter(it had its controversy and currently archived) and one more is last week when i switheed to
the 13 version. since I was not really using that plugin much It isnt much of a issue. I could talk about neovim whole day
about my workflow and all. It'a quite nice editor.(we got mutlicursor too lol).

coding style and taste. I have some problems with LLM for this reason. Thier solution always what would I say
